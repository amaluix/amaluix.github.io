AI consulting in Kerala

I’m Amal Chandran, an AI Generalist based in Kerala. I help businesses assess where AI fits and turn a useful idea into an implementation plan.

AI consulting starts with a business decision: which task is worth improving, what information is available, and where people need to stay involved. My focus spans AI agents, voice AI, workflow automation, product development, and team adoption.

Start with the work

Bring a customer enquiry, a repetitive operational task, or a product idea. We can examine the current workflow before choosing an AI tool. The useful starting point is what needs to change for the people doing the work.

An initial discussion can cover the tools already in place, the information the system may use, the people affected, and the constraints on implementation. A simple integration or a clearer process may be more appropriate than an AI agent.

Define a useful scope

A proposed engagement can move from use-case assessment to a prototype, an integration plan, or training for the team. The exact deliverables, availability, fees, and delivery schedule are agreed after discussing your situation.

  • Choose a specific workflow and the decision it supports.
  • Agree what a useful result would look like before building.
  • Identify data access, system connections, and human review.
  • Test representative situations and decide what needs further work.

Before we talk

What should I prepare?

Describe one task you want to improve, the current tools and people involved, and the outcome you need. An example of the workflow is more useful than a long list of AI features. Avoid sending private customer records in an initial enquiry.

Do I need a finished brief?

No. You can start with a business problem. We can discuss whether it needs an AI product, an automated workflow, a conversational agent, or training for the team.

What does consulting cost?

There is no fixed public price list. Scope, fees, availability, and timelines are confirmed after we discuss the problem and the work involved.
